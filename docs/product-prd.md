# Product Requirements Document

## Product goal

Build the most trusted South African marketplace for finding, comparing, and booking independent mechanics.

## Primary audiences

1. Car owners who need trustworthy repairs without dealer pricing.
2. Independent workshops that want measurable lead generation.
3. Platform admins who must maintain data quality and credibility.

## Key product pillars

- Trust and verification
- Price transparency
- High-intent lead capture
- Mechanic monetization
- Future commerce expansion

## MVP scope

### Consumer
- Homepage
- Search results
- Mechanic detail pages
- Quote request flow
- Fair Price Index

### Provider
- Claim page
- Pricing plans
- Dashboard metrics

### Admin
- Moderation queue
- Accreditation verification
- Pricing anomaly checks

## Monetization

- Free profiles
- Growth subscription
- Pro subscription
- Sponsored listings
- Pay-per-lead

## Future expansion

- Parts and service kits
- Fleet plans
- Roadside assistance
- Pre-purchase inspections
